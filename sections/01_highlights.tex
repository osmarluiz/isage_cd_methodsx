\noindent\textbf{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
  \item Extends iSAGE sparse point supervision to bi-temporal
        change detection.
  \item Labels are anchored to a (reference, frame) pair, not to
        a single image.
  \item Dual synchronized canvases: click on either date lands
        the point on both.
  \item Annotation tree is the dataset itself -- no export step,
        leakage-free splits.
  \item Validated on a real Amazon PRODES 2022 session
        (2000 tiles, 5 iterations).
\end{itemize}
