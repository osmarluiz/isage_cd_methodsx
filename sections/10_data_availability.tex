The iSAGE-CD source code, including the session builder, the
migration tool and the rasteriser, is available at
\url{https://github.com/osmarluiz/isage_cd} under CC BY 4.0. The
imagery of the Amazon session comes from the Planet NICFI
programme~\cite{nicfi2021basemaps} and cannot be redistributed by
the authors. The sparse annotations of the session are available
from the corresponding author on reasonable request, and can be
reused by any team with its own access to the same imagery. The
PRODES reference product, distributed by Brazil's National
Institute for Space Research (INPE), is publicly
available~\cite{prodes}.
