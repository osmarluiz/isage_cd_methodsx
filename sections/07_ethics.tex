% =============================================================
% ETHICS STATEMENTS
% =============================================================

The work reported in this article involved no human subjects and
no animal experiments; the annotation session described in
Section~\ref{sec:val_session} was carried out by one of the
authors, so no ethics review board approval was required.

The PlanetScope imagery used in the Amazon session is subject to
Planet Labs licensing terms and is not redistributed with this
article. The derived artefacts of the session --- the sparse
annotations, the class definitions, and the scripts that
reconstruct the training data --- are shared alongside the tool
and can be reused by any team with an independent PlanetScope
licence. The PRODES deforestation product used as a reference is
a public dataset distributed by Brazil's National Institute for
Space Research (INPE)~\cite{prodes}.

The iSAGE-CD tool is released under the CC BY 4.0 licence at
\url{https://github.com/osmarluiz/isage_cd}.
