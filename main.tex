\title{iSAGE-CD: sparse point supervision for bi-temporal change detection
       with pair-anchored annotations}

\begin{abstract}
Supervised change detection in remote sensing requires per-pixel
labels on two co-registered dates, and producing them is the main
cost of building a training dataset.
iSAGE recently showed that, for single-image semantic segmentation,
expert clicks placed on the model's confident mistakes recover most
of the quality of dense labeling, but the framework does not model
the bi-temporal case where a label is anchored to a pair of dates.
We introduce iSAGE-CD, an extension of iSAGE to change detection
built around three design choices: labels are anchored to a
(reference, frame) pair rather than to a single image; the
annotator shows two synchronized canvases so that a click on
either date lands the point on both; and the annotation tree is
the dataset itself, with no separate export step.
We validate the tool on a working annotation session over a scene
of the Brazilian Amazon covering the PRODES 2022 window
(Aug 2021 -- Jul 2022): 2000 tiles of PlanetScope imagery labeled
by a single expert who ran five iterations of the
click-train-predict loop, placing 143{,}634 points, under
0.007\% of the pixels in the session.
The training loss converges to a stable regime across all
iterations, and the resulting per-tile predictions are
qualitatively coherent with the observable change on
representative tiles.
Full quantitative evaluation against dense supervision and PRODES
is the subject of a separate manuscript in preparation.
iSAGE-CD is released under CC BY 4.0 at
\url{https://github.com/osmarluiz/isage_cd}.
\end{abstract}
